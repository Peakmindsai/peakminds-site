Common Questions

Is PeakMinds AI affiliated with the named advisors?

No. PeakMinds AI simulates advisory perspectives based entirely on publicly available books, podcasts, interviews, and frameworks. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any of the named individuals.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. You can cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings. Your access continues until the end of the current billing period with no penalties.

What is the 7-day money-back guarantee?

If you're not satisfied within the first 7 days of your paid subscription, email us at support@peakmindsai.com and we'll issue a full refund — no questions asked.

How is billing handled?

All payments are securely processed by Paddle, our Merchant of Record. They handle billing, tax compliance, and receipts. You'll see "Paddle" on your bank statement.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), PayPal, and other methods supported by Paddle in your region.
